# Portfolio analysis criteria

## Data sufficiency

- Return: aligned valuations and all material cash flows.
- Volatility and correlation: consistent periodic total-return observations; disclose frequency and sample size.
- Sharpe: return series plus matching risk-free series and convention.
- Liquidity: documented redemption/settlement rule, not asset class alone.
- Credit risk: issuer/guarantor and seniority or rating evidence; absence is unknown, not low risk.

## Benchmark fit

Select benchmark by mandate, currency, geography, duration, risk, and strategy. Use CDI/Selic for appropriate post-fixed BRL exposures, duration/coherent inflation indices for inflation-linked bonds, IFIX for comparable Brazilian real-estate-fund exposure, and relevant total-return equity indices for equity mandates. Explain exceptions.

## Concentration and diversification

Report weights by asset, class, institution, sector, country, and currency. Evaluate correlated exposures and issuer groups. A large asset count does not prove diversification.

## Rebalancing

Compare current weights with approved targets and bands. Prefer new contributions and tax/cost-aware scenarios before sales. Show proposed amounts, resulting weights, liquidity effects, taxes/costs, assumptions, and policy compliance. Never execute the plan.
