# Metrics and benchmark guide

## Financial control

- Savings rate: `(income - expenses) / income`; define whether debt principal and transfers are excluded.
- Investment rate: net investment contributions divided by income for the same period.
- Net worth: assets minus liabilities at one valuation date.
- Reserve coverage: liquid emergency reserve divided by average monthly essential expenses. Report the expense window and liquidity rule.
- Cash flow: cash inflows minus cash outflows; transfers between owned accounts are not income or expense.
- Goal distance: required future value minus current goal-dedicated balance, never below zero for presentation.

## Investment interpretation

- Nominal return ignores inflation; real return uses `(1 + nominal) / (1 + inflation) - 1`.
- Volatility measures return dispersion, not total risk.
- Drawdown measures peak-to-trough loss on a wealth or price series.
- Sharpe requires a coherent risk-free series, return frequency, and enough observations.
- Concentration must be shown by asset and relevant dimensions; effective diversification depends on correlations, not asset count alone.

## Benchmark selection

Use the same dates and currency. Typical candidates, subject to the product mandate:

| Exposure | Candidate benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Post-fixed BRL fixed income | CDI or Selic | Match tax and liquidity context separately. |
| Inflation-linked BRL bonds | IPCA plus stated real-rate objective or duration-matched index | Do not compare short holding periods with maturity yield. |
| Brazilian equities | Ibovespa or a broader/sector index | Choose according to strategy and investable universe. |
| Brazilian real-estate funds | IFIX | Compare total-return conventions consistently. |
| International equities | Relevant country/global total-return index | Convert consistently or show local and BRL views. |
| Cash reserve | CDI/Selic with liquidity and credit constraints | Safety and access can dominate return. |

State when no adequate benchmark is available.

## Projections and goals

Show current value, contribution timing, horizon, nominal/real rate, inflation, taxes/costs, and scenario assumptions. Provide conservative, base, and optimistic cases without implying probability or guarantee unless a documented model supports it.
